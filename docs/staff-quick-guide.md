# Staff quick guide

Print this page and keep it at the front desk.

## Every day

| I want to… | Where |
|---|---|
| Find a patient | Search box at the top, or **Patients** |
| Register a new patient | **Patients** → add. If a possible duplicate appears, open the existing patient instead |
| Book, move or cancel an appointment | **Appointments** |
| Mark a patient arrived | **Appointments** → open the booking → Arrived |
| Write and sign the visit note (dentist) | **Clinical visits** |
| Post a charge or take a payment | **Billing** → open the patient's account |
| Print a receipt | The receipt link next to the payment |
| Give money back | Patient account → Record returned payment (needs permission) |
| Reduce or fix a wrong charge | Patient account → Reduce or correct (owner) |
| Record a clinic expense | **Expenses** |
| Call patients due for recall | **Follow-ups & recalls** |
| Close the day | **Daily closing**: count the cash, save, then a second person approves |

## Rules to remember

- **Nothing is deleted or edited after posting.** A wrong payment is refunded. A wrong charge is corrected with a credit note. This protects you: every change shows who made it.
- **A closed day is locked.** Payments, refunds and expenses cannot be added to it.
- **Sign out or lock the screen when you leave the desk.** The app signs you out after 30 minutes without activity.
- **Never share your password.** Each person has their own login; the owner adds and removes staff on **Staff and access**.
- Forgot your password? Type your email on the sign-in page and press **Forgot password**.

## If the internet or the app is down

1. Keep working on paper. Use one sheet per day with these columns: time, patient name and phone, what was done, amount, how paid (cash, card, bank, Easypaisa, JazzCash), received by.
2. Write appointments made by phone on the same sheet.
3. Do not close the day on paper.
4. When the app is back, one person enters the sheet in order: new patients, then charges, then payments with the **real payment date**. Tick each line as it is entered and staple the sheet into the day's file.
5. If the app is down for more than 30 minutes, tell the owner, who contacts support.
